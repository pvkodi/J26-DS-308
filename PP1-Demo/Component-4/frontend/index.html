Interpretation
Component 4 edits context-dependent request suffixes. The recorded IFBench and IHEval studies are small, selected evaluation cohorts. Official checker passes show rule compliance, not complete answer quality; one IFBench checker can accept an answer that omits substantive content.
Use the recorded examples to inspect exact before-and-after outputs and source checkers. Treat live optimization as a separate local inference path.

        

      

    

  

  
SCOPE & EVIDENCE
Component 4
Stage A records reusable policy versions. Stage B selects a request-specific suffix using the current conversation context and the frozen policy. Recorded examples are visibly separated from live local calls.
Native checker results measure the listed instructions only. They do not certify that every response fulfills the broader user request.
